Local-only Material 3 Android video player powered by libmpv (media_kit). Your videos stay on your device — no cloud, no tracking, no analytics.

<b>Library</b>
• Scans internal storage, SD cards and USB/OTG drives
• Optional hidden files and auto-refresh
• Videos and Folders tabs with search, filters, bookmarks and pins
• Sort by name, date, size, duration or folder
• Copy, cut, paste, rename, delete and share

<b>Player</b>
• Hardware / software / auto decoder
• Two player styles: bottom dialog sheet or YouTube-style watch page
• Play modes: Order, Loop all, Repeat current, Shuffle, No autoplay
• Resume from last position
• Speed control with optional pitch shift
• Double-tap seek, pinch zoom, brightness & volume gestures
• Many screen modes (Fit, Zoom, Original, Stretch, 16:9, 4:3, 21:9…)
• Picture-in-Picture and in-app mini player
• Background playback with notification controls
• A-B repeat, sleep timer, lock controls
• Screenshot, color correction, night mode, extra dim

<b>Audio</b>
• 10-band equalizer with presets
• Bass boost, surround and left/right balance

<b>Theme & Accessibility</b>
• Light / Dark / System + Material You dynamic color
• High contrast, grayscale, invert, color-blind filters
• Reduce motion, large controls, bold text

<b>Network</b>
The app has no connection on start. It only contacts api.github.com when you open About (developer profile), the changelog page, or tap Check for update. Nothing else is sent.

<b>Permissions</b>
Video access is asked on start. All files access is asked only when you delete, rename, copy or move files.
